Our storyKisah kami

Heritage recipes, a modern room.Resipi warisan, ruang moden.

Rumbia is the sago palm. Its starch becomes ambuyat, the dish at the heart of every Bruneian gathering. We named the restaurant after it.Rumbia ialah pokok sagu. Kanjinya menjadi ambuyat, hidangan di tengah setiap perhimpunan orang Brunei. Restoran ini dinamakan sempena pokok itu.

Our staff are happy to explain every dish. First time trying ambuyat? Ask us, we show you how.Staf kami sedia menerangkan setiap hidangan. Kali pertama cuba ambuyat? Tanya kami, kami tunjukkan caranya.

Business lunches, family dinners and guests from abroad all welcome. Card payment accepted.Makan tengah hari perniagaan, makan malam keluarga dan tetamu luar negara semua dialu-alukan. Bayaran kad diterima.
